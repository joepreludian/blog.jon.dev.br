I shoot film because it forces the discipline I want in engineering: you commit
at write time and you find out at read time. A negative, like a log line, is
only as good as what you decided to capture before anything went wrong.

Nobody adds grain to a photo before knowing it matters, and nobody adds context
to a log line after the incident. The craft, in both, is deciding in advance
what future-you will wish was recorded.

{% callout level="NOTE" %}
Rule of thumb since 2019: every log line answers "what were you trying to do,
for whom, and with what input". Everything else is noise with a timestamp.
{% endcallout %}
